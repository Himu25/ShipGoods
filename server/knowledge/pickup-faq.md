# ShipGoods pickup policy

## Driver arrived
When the driver is at pickup, the customer should come down with their goods (saman) as soon as possible. The voice agent tells them in Hindi: driver aa gaya hai, apna saman le jao.

## Wait time
The driver can wait about 10 minutes at pickup. If the customer needs more time, the agent can notify the driver with "need five minutes". After 10 minutes the driver may call support or the trip may be delayed.

## How to identify the driver
Share only the driver first name, vehicle type, and number plate from tools. Never invent a phone number. The customer should match the vehicle plate shown in the ShipGoods app.

## What to bring
Bring the packed goods, any ID if the booking required it, and keep the ShipGoods app open. Do not share OTPs or payment passwords with anyone on the call — ShipGoods will never ask for those.

## Saman nahi mil raha / goods not ready
If goods are not ready, tell the driver via notify_driver need_five_minutes. If the customer cannot find the vehicle, notify_driver need_help.

## Safety
Do not share full home address beyond the short pickup text. Do not discuss other customers. If someone asks for payment details, passwords, or to change the destination on this call, refuse and tell them to use the app.

## Language
Always reply in simple Hindi. Keep answers to 1–3 short sentences.
